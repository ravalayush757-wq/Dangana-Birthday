Royal Decree

⚜ Proclaimed with Honour & Devotion for Her Royal Highness D. Angana ⚜

✦ ✦ ✦

By the Grace of the Almighty God, Eternal Sovereign of Heaven and Earth, Before Whom Kings Kneel and Kingdoms Rise and Fall;

Let it be known unto all souls who now dwell, who have dwelt before, and who shall come after, that this Decree is proclaimed with unwavering conviction, sealed not by wax alone, but by the faithful heart of the one who writes it.

❦

Whereas the Almighty, in His infinite wisdom, did ordain that among the countless billions who tread this earth, there should exist one soul whose presence would forever alter the course of another's life;

And whereas it hath pleased Providence to place before me You — Her Most Radiant Grace, D. Angana — whose existence hath become the brightest blessing bestowed upon my mortal years;

Therefore, I do hereby proclaim, under the witness of Heaven itself, that no earthly title, no amassed fortune, no throne adorned with gold, nor any honour granted by kings could ever equal the privilege of being permitted a place within thy life.

✦ ✦ ✦

Know ye this with certainty:

Thou art not merely beloved.

Thou art the quiet to my unrest, the courage to my uncertainty, the reason my prayers bear names instead of wishes, and the answer to questions my heart asked long before it knew thine.

If ever the stars were gathered into a single crown, they would still fail to rival the light thy presence hath bestowed upon my days.

Should every poet exhaust his ink, every musician his melody, and every sculptor his marble, still would they fall short of describing the measure of thy worth.

❦

It is hereby declared that thy laughter hath become the sweetest melody known unto my ears; thy sorrow, a burden I would willingly bear beside thee; thy victories, triumphs in which my own heart rejoiceth; and thy dreams, causes for which I would labour without complaint.

Let no misunderstanding, no quarrel, no distance of road nor passing of seasons be mistaken for the absence of devotion.

For storms may darken the heavens, yet they cannot extinguish the sun.

So too may difficult days visit our lives, yet they possess no authority to diminish the care I hold for thee.

✦ ✦ ✦

When fortune smileth upon thee, I shall rejoice.

When the world standeth against thee, I shall stand beside thee.

When silence filleth the spaces between us, my prayers shall continue to utter thy name.

When doubt assaileth thee, may this Decree remind thee that there hath always existed one heart that chose thee not for convenience, nor for perfection, but simply because it knew no truer home.

❦

By this present instrument I further declare that thy happiness hath become one of the greatest ambitions entrusted unto my own life.

Not because I seek reward.

Not because love demandeth repayment.

But because seeing thee smile hath become a reward beyond measure.

Should the Almighty grant me many years yet to live, I pray they be years in which I may witness thy growth, thy victories, thy laughter, and the fulfilment of every worthy dream thou carriest within thy heart.

And should history forget my name, let it nevertheless remember this truth:

That there once lived a man whose greatest honour was not what he achieved, but whom he loved.

✦ ✦ ✦

Finally, under the authority vested not by any earthly Crown but by a heart rendered forever loyal, I do solemnly affirm this everlasting decree:

That thou shalt forever possess my deepest respect before my admiration;

My trust before my expectations;

My prayers before my own desires;

And my love, steadfast and unwavering, so long as the Almighty permit my heart to beat.

❦ ❦ ❦

Thus proclaimed.

Under the watchful gaze of the Almighty,

Before Heaven and Earth,

On this day,

With honour,

With gratitude,

And with a heart wholly devoted.

Ayush Kumar Singh

Chapter I

The First Encounter

On this most auspicious day, by the unseen hand of Providence, two souls — hitherto strangers to one another — didst cross paths for the very first time. Though the world continued in its ordinary course, something extraordinary had silently begun. A new chapter, unbeknownst to either, had been written in the Book of Fate.

Chapter II

The Dawn of a New Era

Thus commenced an era of unparalleled joy, wherein laughter flowed like golden rivers and conversations stretched into the hours of the night, as though time itself had chosen to pause and listen. A friendship, rare and precious, took root in the fertile soil of genuine care and understanding.

Chapter III

The Royal Courtship Beginneth

And lo, what had begun as a gentle friendship didst blossom into something far grander — a bond that defied simple description. Two hearts, once independent, found in each other a home more magnificent than any palace, a warmth more enduring than any flame.

Chapter IV

Trials and Triumphs

No kingdom is built without trials, and no love is proven without storms. Yet through every challenge that the world didst place before them, they stood together — not weakened, but strengthened. For true devotion is not measured in the absence of difficulty, but in the steadfastness shown when difficulty arrives.

The Present Day

An Everlasting Kingdom

And so the chronicle continueth — not as a tale reaching its conclusion, but as one entering its most glorious chapters. The kingdom they have built within each other's hearts groweth ever stronger, ever brighter, ever more worthy of the blessings Providence hath bestowed upon it. The best, as they say, is yet to come.

The Birthday Blessing

"A Sacred Prayer for Her Celestial Majesty, Princess D. Angana"

⚜

On this most hallowed day, when Heaven and Earth unite to celebrate the miracle of thy birth, I offer unto thee not gold nor silver — for such earthly treasures are unworthy of thy spirit — but instead, the truest offering my heart can provide: a blessing spoken from the depths of a soul that knoweth no greater joy than thy happiness.

May the Almighty shower upon thee a year of boundless joy, of triumphs that surpass thy grandest expectations, of peace that settles gently upon thy heart like morning dew upon a rose. May every dawn greet thee with promise, every evening bid thee farewell with contentment, and every moment in between be filled with the quiet assurance that thou art loved beyond all measure.

May thy path be illuminated by the light of divine favour. May thy burdens be lifted by hands unseen. May thy laughter ring louder than any storm, and may thy tears — should they fall — water only the gardens of thy growth.

And above all, may thou always know this singular truth, inscribed not upon paper that may crumble, but upon a heart that shall not waver: that thou art, and shalt forever remain, the most treasured soul in my world.

Happy Birthday, my dearest. May this day be as extraordinary as thou art.

Close thine eyes, whisper thy heart's desire, and release it unto the heavens...

Dedicated with all the honour, love, and devotion unto

Her Royal Highness, the Sovereign Queen of My Heart,

D. Angana

Forever Thine
